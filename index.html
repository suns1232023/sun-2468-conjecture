This work does not claim a proof of the Sun (2,4,6,8) Binomial Representation Conjecture. It develops a computationally audited proof architecture and reduces the remaining difficulty to a sharply formulated analytic closure problem.

The Conjecture

The Sun (2,4,6,8) Binomial Representation Conjecture states that every sufficiently large integer admits a representation of the form

\[ n= \binom{w}{2} + \binom{x}{4} + \binom{y}{6} + \binom{z}{8} \] \[ w\ge2,\qquad x\ge4,\qquad y\ge6,\qquad z\ge8. \]

Abstract

We study Sun's (2,4,6,8) binomial representation conjecture and develop a witness-geometric reformulation through global crossing transfer and defect-capacity analysis.

The principal contributions of Version V15.6 are:

  • Exact identity \[ w^*(n)=K(n)+\Delta(n)+2. \]
  • Identification of the extremal transfer constant \[ C_T=\frac{64}{19}. \]
  • Verification of the subcritical constant \[ C_K= \frac{118202}{59375} <2. \]
  • Exhaustive computational verification up to \[ N=2,000,000. \]
  • Reduction of the remaining difficulty to an analytic closure problem concerning short-interval coverage of \[ H=S_4+S_6+S_8. \]
