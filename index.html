1. The Conjecture

In 2019, Professor Zhi-Wei Sun (Nanjing University) proposed the (2,4,6,8) Binomial Representation Conjecture ( OEIS A306477 ).

Every positive integer \(N\) can be represented in the mixed-degree binomial sum form:

\[ N = \binom{w}{2} + \binom{x}{4} + \binom{y}{6} + \binom{z}{8} \] \[ w \ge 2, \qquad x \ge 4, \qquad y \ge 6, \qquad z \ge 8. \]

2. Key Theoretical Results (Framework V13.1)

  • Triangular Witness Trajectories & Gap-Cascade Bounds: Introduces deterministic trajectory-crossing conditions and gap-cascade bounds, quantifying structural coverage across high-density sumset intersections.
  • Sub-Square-Root Gap Growth: Exhaustive computation up to \(10^{12}\) bounds the maximum gap as \[ \operatorname{MaxGap}(X) \approx 0.9841\,X^{0.4789}. \]
  • Rigid Zero-Set \(\mathcal{E}_8\): Confirmed permanent exception set \[ \mathcal{E}_8 = \{1,2,3,5,7,11\}. \] The set is reported as strictly invariant for \(X \ge 10^4\).
